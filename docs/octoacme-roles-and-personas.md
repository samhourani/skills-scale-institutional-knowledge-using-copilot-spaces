# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Owners and Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Business Owners and Stakeholders define the strategic outcomes and business priorities that guide project work. They ensure projects remain aligned with organizational goals, customer needs, and value expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, measurable outcomes, and success criteria
- Sponsor project direction and approve scope, priorities, and major trade-offs
- Provide business context and requirements to project teams
- Review deliverables for business alignment and adoption readiness
- Escalate decisions or constraints that affect enterprise priorities

### Goals
- Ensure projects produce measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between delivery work and strategic goals
- Support clear executive decision-making across the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee meetings and executive updates
- Review sessions for scope, milestone acceptance, and outcome validation
- Prioritization and change approval discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to confirm scope, milestones, and risks
- Provides direction to Product Managers on desired business outcomes and trade-offs
- Collaborates with Developers and Technical Leads to confirm feasibility and business impact
- Aligns with Risk Officers to ensure key business risks are identified and managed

---

## Technical Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects shape the technical strategy, solution design, and implementation standards for the project. They bridge business needs and engineering execution while reducing technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define the technical approach, architecture, and implementation standards
- Review solution design, dependencies, and technical trade-offs
- Guide engineering teams on patterns, quality expectations, and maintainability
- Identify technical risks, blockers, and mitigation strategies
- Support release readiness and technical sign-off decisions

### Goals
- Deliver a technically sound and maintainable solution
- Reduce avoidable architecture and implementation risk
- Align engineering practices with organizational standards

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Dependency planning and technical risk reviews
- Technical sign-off or release checkpoints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align technical milestones and dependencies with delivery plans
- Collaborates with Product Managers and Business Owners to connect business priorities to feasible technical solutions
- Guides Developers on design standards, implementation constraints, and quality expectations
- Coordinates with QA Leads to ensure testing approaches match architecture and risk areas

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and oversee testing strategy, quality standards, and validation activities. They ensure that delivered work meets acceptance criteria and is ready for stakeholder confidence and release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and acceptance validation approach
- Coordinate test planning, execution, and defect triage
- Validate that releases meet functional and non-functional quality requirements
- Track quality trends, recurring risks, and release blockers
- Partner with delivery teams to improve testing quality and efficiency

### Goals
- Verify product quality before release and stakeholder acceptance
- Reduce defects that affect customer trust or release readiness
- Improve confidence in delivery through repeatable quality practices

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions and quality reviews
- Defect triage and release readiness check-ins
- Validation summaries for project leads and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides validation feedback to Developers and Technical Leads on quality gaps and remediation needs
- Aligns with Product Managers and Business Owners on acceptance criteria and readiness for launch
- Works closely with Project Managers to surface quality risks that could affect timeline or deployment decisions
- Supports Risk Officers in identifying risks related to defects, compliance, or operational impact

---

## Risk Officers and Process Auditors

### Role Summary
Risk Officers and Process Auditors identify, monitor, and escalate project risks, compliance concerns, and process gaps. They support continuous improvement by making risks visible and helping teams act before issues escalate.

### Responsibilities
- Identify project risks across schedule, scope, quality, dependencies, and stakeholder alignment
- Facilitate risk assessment, mitigation planning, and escalation paths
- Monitor adherence to process standards and project governance expectations
- Capture lessons learned and recommend improvements to project practices
- Support retrospectives and ongoing process refinement

### Goals
- Reduce uncertainty and prevent avoidable delivery issues
- Strengthen governance and operational consistency across projects
- Improve organizational learning and process maturity over time

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews, issue escalation updates, and governance checkpoints
- Retrospective findings and process improvement discussions
- Weekly or milestone-based reporting to project leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to track mitigation actions and escalation status
- Advises Business Owners and Product Managers on risk trade-offs and decision impacts
- Coordinates with Technical Leads and QA Leads to understand delivery and quality risks
- Helps teams connect retrospective insights to process updates and future project planning

---

## Communication and Documentation Specialists

### Role Summary
Communication and Documentation Specialists maintain clear information flow, alignment artifacts, and institutional knowledge across the project lifecycle. They ensure teams, sponsors, and stakeholders have the information they need to make timely and informed decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain project communication plans, status narratives, and decision logs
- Document key decisions, trade-offs, and stakeholder updates
- Support onboarding, knowledge transfer, and continuity across project phases
- Ensure documentation is available, consistent, and accessible to the right audiences
- Coordinate recurring updates for internal and external stakeholders

### Goals
- Improve clarity, traceability, and stakeholder confidence
- Preserve institutional knowledge across project transitions and handoffs
- Support consistent communication across project teams and leadership

### Typical Communication
- Weekly update summaries and stakeholder briefings
- Decision logs, meeting notes, and process documentation
- Knowledge-sharing sessions and onboarding materials

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Project Managers maintain clear status reporting and consistent documentation
- Supports Product Managers and Business Owners with stakeholder communication and project narrative
- Works with Technical Leads and QA Leads to translate technical details into shared understanding across audiences
- Strengthens the communication flow between delivery teams and executive stakeholders

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles make it easier to model accountability, collaboration, and decision-making across the full project lifecycle.
